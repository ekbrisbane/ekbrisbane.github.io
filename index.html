About

A legal career, built twice.

From European civil law practice in Istanbul to family and fertility law in Brisbane.

Two legal traditions, one profession, and a practice centred on how families are formed and recognised.

i

Brisbane · 2026Family and fertility law

I joined Page Provan Family and Fertility Lawyers in July 2026 and was admitted as a lawyer of the Supreme Court of Queensland the same year. I work directly with director Stephen Page in surrogacy, fertility and family law, including matters in Queensland, New South Wales and Victoria.

ii

Queensland · 2021 to 2026Rebuilding in Australian law

My Australian career began on the Gold Coast at Gall Standfield & Smith, in wills, estates and conveyancing. I then joined the External Interventions team at the Queensland Law Society, working inside the profession’s regulator.

Alongside full-time work I completed the required law subjects at QUT and USQ, then the Graduate Diploma of Legal Practice, with practical training in criminal law at Ashworth Lawyers.

iii

At the end of 2019 I left Istanbul for Australia. My overseas law degree was assessed for admission in Queensland, which set out the Australian law subjects I would need to complete.

iv

İstanbul · 2014 to 2019Training in the civil law

I studied law at Maltepe University and gained my first experience as a summer clerk at Esenyel & Partners, a maritime and commercial firm. After the statutory year of bar traineeship, I was admitted to the Istanbul Bar in 2018.

Areas of focus

Where family, medicine and law meet.

A description of the work I do at Page Provan. Every matter turns on its own facts and on the law of the relevant state or territory.

i

Surrogacy law

Working with intended parents and birth parents on domestic and cross-border surrogacy, from first advice through to court orders.

  • Legal frameworks and eligibility
  • Surrogacy arrangements
  • Parentage orders
  • Cross-border considerations

Jurisdiction noteEach state and territory has its own surrogacy legislation, and commercial surrogacy is prohibited throughout Australia. In Queensland parentage orders are made by the Childrens Court; in Victoria, substitute parentage orders are made by the County Court or Supreme Court.

ii

Fertility and donor conception

The legal questions that arise from assisted reproduction, for intended parents, donors and recipients.

  • Known donor arrangements
  • Intended parenthood
  • Legal parentage considerations
  • Stored gametes and embryos

Jurisdiction noteWho is a legal parent of a donor-conceived child depends on state and territory status of children laws and the Family Law Act 1975 (Cth). Some states, including New South Wales and Victoria, regulate assisted reproductive treatment through dedicated legislation; Queensland relies on national clinical guidelines.

iii

Family law

Parenting and property matters after separation, including the steps that come before any court proceedings.

  • Parenting matters
  • Property settlements
  • Binding financial agreements
  • Dispute resolution and court processes

Jurisdiction noteFamily law is largely national under the Family Law Act 1975 (Cth), with matters heard in the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia. Western Australia has its own Family Court.

General information about my areas of work, not legal advice. Legal services are provided by Page Provan Family and Fertility Lawyers.
