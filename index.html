About Evren Kurt

  • Families.
  • Parenthood.
  • Relationships.
  • Recognition.

I am a lawyer at Page Provan Family and Fertility Lawyers, a Brisbane firm practising in family, surrogacy and fertility law. I work closely with director Stephen Page on surrogacy and fertility matters for intended parents, birth parents and donors in Queensland, New South Wales and Victoria, and on family law matters including parenting, property and financial agreements.

Before joining Page Provan I worked in private practice on the Gold Coast and in the regulatory team of the Queensland Law Society. I first qualified and practised as a lawyer in İstanbul, and I work in English, Turkish and French.

I practise as an employed lawyer. Legal services are provided by Page Provan Family and Fertility Lawyers ↗
